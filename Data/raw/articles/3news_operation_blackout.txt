Summary: Cyber Security Authority Rejects Portrayal of Operation Blackout as FBI-Led

Source: 3News Ghana
Author: Laud Nartey
Publication date: 9 October 2026

Main summary:

The Cyber Security Authority (CSA) rejected portrayals of Operation Blackout, also known as Operation Cyber Hunt, as an operation independently conceived, commanded, or executed by the United States Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI). According to the CSA, Ghanaian authorities planned, coordinated, and executed the operation within Ghana's legal and institutional framework.

The CSA identified several participating institutions, including Ghana's National Security Council, the Ghana Police Service's Criminal Investigations Department, the Ghana Immigration Service, the Office of the Attorney-General, the FBI, the United Kingdom's National Crime Agency, and Nigeria's Economic and Financial Crimes Commission. It acknowledged the intelligence contributions of international partners while emphasizing that their roles should be represented accurately.

The authority stressed Ghana's sovereignty, stating that foreign law-enforcement agencies cannot independently exercise police powers or conduct arrests within Ghana. It called on the FBI to clarify its public account and recognize the contributions of Ghanaian institutions and other international partners.

According to the article, the operation uncovered suspected cybercrime centres in the Greater Accra Region. Eight suspects were arrested, and 120 people suspected of being trafficked and exploited for online fraud were rescued. Authorities reportedly seized 136 mobile phones, 130 laptops, internet devices, four vehicles, and other items. Investigations and efforts to repatriate the rescued victims were ongoing.

Main narrative: The article presents the operation as Ghana-led, with international agencies contributing intelligence and operational support.

Sentiment and framing: The coverage emphasizes national sovereignty, Ghanaian institutional leadership, lawful cooperation, and accurate recognition of participating agencies. Its tone is formal and explanatory, while the CSA's response challenges the portrayal of the operation as independently FBI-led.

Data note: This is a research summary of the 3News article, not the full original text. Claims about leadership, arrests, and rescued victims are attributed to the CSA as reported by 3News.
