# Summary: FBI's Account of Operation Blackout in Ghana

**Source:** Citi Newsroom  
**Article:** Kash Patel acknowledges Ghana in FBI's global scam-centre crackdown  
**Publication date:** 12 October 2026

## Main Summary

FBI Director Kash Patel presented Operation Blackout as part of a wider FBI campaign against scam-centre fraud. In a public post, he described the operation as continuing to produce results in Ghana and highlighted arrests, seizures and efforts to protect victims of fraud.

Citi Newsroom reported that the FBI's initial account on 8 October 2026 said more than 130 people had been arrested or detained in Ghana. The initial account also referred to nearly US$10 million in identified losses and 89 victims. The article noted that Patel's earlier account did not clearly highlight Ghana's role in the operation.

Following criticism from Ghanaian authorities, Patel's later post explicitly referred to Ghana as one of the locations where the FBI's wider campaign was producing results.

## Main Narrative and Framing

The FBI's public framing placed the Ghana operation within its broader international campaign against scam centres and emphasized enforcement results. Ghana's Cyber Security Authority disputed the impression that the operation was independently led by the FBI, maintaining that Ghanaian authorities planned, coordinated and executed it with international support.
