TITLE: CSA clarifies Ghana’s leadership of “Operation Blackout”

PUBLICATION DATE: 9 October 2026

ORIGINAL SOURCE:

RESEARCH SUMMARY:

The Cyber Security Authority states that Ghanaian authorities planned, coordinated and executed Operation Blackout, also known as Operation Cyber Hunt. It disputes portrayals of the operation as independently led or commanded by the FBI.

The CSA describes cooperation between Ghanaian institutions and international law-enforcement partners, including the FBI and the United Kingdom’s National Crime Agency. It emphasizes Ghana’s legal authority over law-enforcement operations conducted within its territory.

The authority acknowledges contributions from international partners while arguing that public accounts should accurately recognize the roles of Ghanaian institutions and other agencies involved. It calls for clarification of the public account of the operation.
